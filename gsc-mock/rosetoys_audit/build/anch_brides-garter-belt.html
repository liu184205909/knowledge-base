Brides Garter Belt

HomeBrides Garter Belt

No single bridal accessory carries as much history as the brides garter belt. Tossed at receptions for centuries, it is the one piece of the wedding look guests actually cheer for — and the one only two people see up close. A good garter is a wide band of lace or satin that slides to the upper thigh, holds without pinching, and carries a tiny something blue when tradition calls. Modern brides often wear two: one to keep, one to throw. This guide covers how to size the band, where it sits, what it clips to, and how the whole ritual plays out.

Picking the Right Brides Garter Belt

Size is everything with a garter: measure around the mid-thigh where the band will sit, and choose one with a touch of stretch lace so it stays put through dinner and dancing. Width sets the tone — a slim satin band reads minimal and modern, while wide lace with ribbon detail reads classic bridal. Blue charms, pearls, or a small bow cover the something-blue without a second thought. Slip it on just before the reception, over bare skin or over sheer stockings, and wear the second one higher for the toss.

The right garter deserves the right company: sheer stockings it can clip to, and the finishing touches around it.
