Fishnet stockings are the fastest way to take an outfit from simple to statement, and this collection stretches the classic far beyond basic black. Choose thigh high fishnet stockings to wear with garter belts, skirts, and lingerie sets, or go head to toe with fishnet dresses and bodystockings. Color sets the tone: black fishnet stays cool and versatile for everyday wear, while a true red turns the heat up for date nights. The open weave flatters every body type, with plenty of forgiving stretch that hugs your shape instead of squeezing it.

How to Style Fishnet Stockings

Start with the net itself: classic fishnet is the everyday go-to, fine micro-net reads subtle under tailored pants, and larger fence-net weaves make the boldest impact. Finer weaves read closer to a sheer tight; wider diamonds stretch further and read bolder. Layering is where fishnet shines: wear thigh high fishnet stockings over opaque tights for winter texture, or pair them with shorts for lived-in rock edge. To keep the open weave snag-free, hand wash in cool water and dry flat. For a softer counterpoint, contrast your fishnet with delicate lace from our stockings collection.
