### L3 · 设计师 spec 指南帖（排期槽：W2 周一 10-19）

**图**：`https://cushionmill.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cm-sunbrella-vs-outdoor-fabric-23043588.png`

```
"Firm" is not a specification.

Ask five workshops for a "firm" cushion and you'll get five different feels. The upholsterers' forums have the right vocabulary — foam spec, stated as density and compression — but almost none of it reaches the customer-side order form.

If you write cushion specs for clients, three numbers do the work:

1. Foam density (lb/ft³) — how long the cushion holds up. 2.8-3 lb is the range that still feels right in year five; 1.8 lb is the replacement-cycle business model.
2. Compression / ILD (approx. 35-40 lb for a supportive seat) — how it feels on day one. Density and firmness are independent variables; confusing them is how projects get "firm" foam that still goes flat.
3. Thickness — set by the seat frame and desired sitting height, not by preference.

A spec with those three numbers is reproducible by any workshop, on any reorder, years later. A spec that says "firm, medium-firm-ish" is a coin flip.

We build to written specs — density, compression, thickness — across 119 fabrics. Standard 4-7 business days, rush 3-5.

Spec sheet questions welcome: https://cushionmill.com/custom-cushions/
```
